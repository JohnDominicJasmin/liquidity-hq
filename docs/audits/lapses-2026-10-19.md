## How we work

| # | Lapse | Evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | **Nothing watches the data** | Five alert types (EMA 1m, 5m, 1h, 2h, 1d) stopped logging at the same second, 2026-09-27 17:26:15 UTC, unnoticed for 3 weeks; the econ-calendar source (Finnhub) returns nothing for the production key and the route used to drop that silently | A daily automatic health check that compares expected vs actual activity and posts a failure where the team sees it (#1594); #1584 |
