## The pitch (one line)

**"Read the map. Hunt the stops."** — professional-grade crypto intelligence for retail traders, built around the idea that retail traders get liquidated because they can't see what institutions see. This app shows them the map.

## Who it's for

Retail crypto traders — people trading BTC/ETH/alts on leverage, checking charts daily, active in Telegram groups and crypto Twitter, the kind of trader who gets rekt by a stop hunt and wants to know *why* it happened and how to see it coming next time.

## What the product actually does (current features)

1. **AI Arena** — the flagship feature. A 35-signal confluence engine with a live chart: funding rate, CVD, open interest trend, squeeze score, whale flow, GEX (gamma exposure), all in one view. You pick a coin, hit Analyze, and Grok (xAI's model) reads all 35 signals and gives a direct trade bias — not a vague "could go either way," an actual call.
2. **Telegram Alerts** — auto-fires for squeeze setups, whale trades, RSI extremes, EMA crosses, rapid price moves, OI spikes, and breaking news, straight to your phone.
3. **Morning Briefing** — daily macro snapshot: BTC dominance, dollar index (DXY), S&P correlation, ETF flows, economic calendar, hot setups. Know the backdrop before the session opens.
4. **News Feed** — Reuters, AP, Al Jazeera, Fox News, Politico, CoinDesk and more, auto-classified and scored for crypto relevance, ~1 min lag on breaking news.
5. **Whale Tracker** — real-time large-trade detection across Binance and Bybit, configurable per-coin thresholds.
6. **Squeeze Scanner** — scans all 17 tracked coins simultaneously for 3-signal squeeze setups (funding rate + long/short ratio + taker pressure). When all three align, a flush is coming.
7. **Macro regime badge** (just shipped) — most tools just show "DXY up = bad for crypto." That's wrong: DXY is 57.6% Euro and only 13.6% yen, so DXY can fall on Euro strength while the yen is actually strengthening — which is a carry-trade unwind and *also* bad for crypto. This badge reads both signals together and tells you the real regime: Cheap Money / Strong Dollar / Carry Unwind Risk / Expensive Money. This is a genuinely differentiated, non-obvious insight — most retail content gets this wrong.
8. **Also included**: price alerts, position sizer, trade journal, liquidation map, funding-rate history, BTC correlation tool, "best hours to trade" tool.
